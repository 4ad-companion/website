The complete v1 toolset

Keep the campaign organised and spend less time managing paper at the table.

Parties and adventurers

Manage multiple parties of up to four adventurers. Keep each hero’s class, Life, Level, XP, Gold, equipment, inventory, notes, statuses, and custom trackers together.

Resources and notes

Track shared supplies with custom party resources, restore the whole party in one tap, and build a dated notes timeline for every adventure.

Dungeon mapping

Draw rooms, corridors, entrances, and doors on a flexible grid. Name locations, add notes, and mark each area as you explore.

Sessions and encounters

Link a party and dungeon to each session, move through mapped locations, and keep rounds, remaining foes, notes, and a session log in one view.

Dice tools

Make quick or custom rolls, attach them to the right adventurer, room, or encounter, and keep the result history with your session.

Search everything

Search parties, adventurers, notes, dungeons, locations, sessions, encounters, and logs—then jump straight back into play.

Your adventure at a glance

Manage adventurers, trackers, and notes in clear layouts made for play.
