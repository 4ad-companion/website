4AD: Companion does not collect, use, or share personal information that identifies you. The app does not require an account, and we do not use user information for advertising, tracking, analytics profiles, or sale to third parties.

## Diagnostic data

The only data that may be collected is diagnostic data that is not linked to your identity:

- Crash data
- Performance data
- Other diagnostic data

This diagnostic data is used only to diagnose app issues, fix crashes, and improve app performance and reliability. It may include technical details such as app version, device model, operating system version, crash reports, performance measurements, and error information.

## App data

4AD: Companion is designed for tracking your party and play session details on your device. That user-created app content is not collected by us for diagnostics, advertising, or profiling.

## Third-party sharing

We do not sell personal information, track you across apps or websites, or share user information with advertisers. Diagnostic data may be processed through Apple-provided app distribution and diagnostics services so we can understand crashes and performance problems.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
